Where Tokyo's Companies Share an Address

Side project, November 2026

Python, pandas, GSI geocoding API, Folium

Each circle is one address, sized by how many companies are registered there. Open the full map.

The idea

While working on my thesis, I kept running into single buildings where hundreds of companies are registered. This map shows where those addresses are in Tokyo.

What I did

  1. Started from 1,173,931 active Tokyo companies in the government's gBizINFO registry.
  2. Normalized the addresses and counted how many companies are registered at each one.
  3. Geocoded the [300] busiest addresses with the free address search from Japan's Geospatial Information Authority (国土地理院).
  4. Plotted them with Folium, with each circle sized by company count.

What stands out

  • The busiest single address, 東京都渋谷区道玄坂1-10-8 in Shibuya, has 5,133 companies registered at it.
  • The hotspots sit in Tokyo's usual business hubs: Shinjuku, Shibuya, Minato and Chiyoda.
  • What surprised me most was how dense the area around the Imperial Palace is. My guess is that it's about more than being close to Tokyo Station. For many large Japanese companies, a Marunouchi or Otemachi address carries prestige and history, and being near business partners, and even competitors, seems to matter more than the cost.

What it doesn't show

A shared address isn't a sign of wrongdoing. Most of these are virtual offices, accounting firms that administer companies, or structured-finance vehicles that are registered side by side by design. For compliance work, the map shows where to look more closely, not what to conclude.
